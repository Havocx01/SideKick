## What changed

`diagnostic_checks.py` composes six diagnostic types: score/persistence inspection, fitted preprocessing audit, descriptive sensor quality, within-model threshold sensitivity, recorded clean-prediction attribution and equipment/fold variation. Each proposed question selects a model, compatible observable discriminator and inspected fault patterns. Sidekick supplies the procedure, controls, observable signal and corresponding conditional outcomes. AI display titles are not published; diagnostic labels come from the server. This prevents an unsupported cause assertion in a title from being displayed as a finding.

Availability is computed for the actual cited model and patterns, after input-budget omissions. Incomplete records produce **Collect evidence first**, with the required records listed. Selected paired summaries are not a population or complete threshold-sweep dataset. Threshold sensitivity always requires complete development traces and the early-alarm eligibility mask, and holds model, preprocessing and alert rules fixed. Sensor quality may include reserved equipment and remains descriptive; its controls explicitly prohibit tuning from that profile.

The guards reject known invalid synchronized-threshold comparisons, neural-layer proposals, model/citation mismatches, unavailable-data causal inferences, unsupported numerical/proof/robustness claims and original-profile assertions that rule out injected-fault response. Neither a score crossing nor consistent preprocessing confirms a cause. Scope/format/citation failures reject the report; independently unsafe explanations/questions are removed. If no safe question remains, the evidence-only fallback applies. If a question survives without any explanation, the result is clearly labeled as a proposed diagnostic, with no fabricated hypothesis.

Historical reports remain readable. New optional test metadata defaults to unknown availability for old records. Contract source participates in cache currency; reopening an old answer does not automatically purchase regeneration. Briefs and exports preserve prerequisites and fixed controls. The existing ownership, consent and revocation rules remain in force.

## Added value versus regular analysis

Regular comparison now prioritizes lr2's failing dropout case regardless of candidate order and already provides the measured shortfall, missing-reading flags, median imputation and late/missed checks. Credit that improvement to deterministic action ranking, not AI.

The retained AI contribution is a focused question: determine whether the affected development scores remain below their own threshold or cross it without warning activation. The conditional interpretation and scope controls are supplied by Sidekick. This can organize an investigation, but the necessary lr2 traces and historical fitted preprocessing are absent from this benchmark. It cannot be executed or establish a mechanism from the aggregate counts. The incremental benefit is **limited and unproved in engineer use**, rather than a new finding or a more accurate model verdict.

The samples are repeated debugging contexts from one benchmark, reviewed by one Codex assessor. No blinded engineer, review-time measurement or real-equipment trial was involved. A next evaluation should compare regular versus AI-assisted review on representative recorded contexts, count invalid/removed explanations and fallback separately, and measure decision correctness, required corrections and time. Do not upgrade the model or claim a benefit percentage from these four attempts alone.
