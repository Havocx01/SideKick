# Shared experiment context and refined reasoning

## Outcome

Equipment type, sensor meaning and the intended decision now belong to the optional pretraining Pilot brief. They save into separate SQLite metadata, not recorded model/scoring evidence. Later edits are available in the collapsed Experiment context section of investigation setup. A focused optional question belongs to each investigation. Reading or editing context makes no AI request; Start investigation is the explicit admission action. Reviewing engineer/contact fields, review procedure and success measures are never copied into AI context.

The Pilot brief retains its original equipment/decision capacities (200/2,000 characters); sensor meaning is limited to 500 and the per-investigation focus to 500. Requests snapshot the shared fields so subsequent edits cannot rewrite a saved analysis. Empty focus keeps legacy reuse identity compatible. Full-local mode and existing origin guards protect context editing. Existing evidence and public mode boundaries remain intact.

## Reasoning fixes

Earlier synthesis required at least one explanation. The production schema now allows an empty explanation list rather than forcing speculation. Its prompt prefers a short possible mechanism and a genuinely competing alternative, with a diagnostic to distinguish them. The report distinguishes deliberate abstention from rejected explanations. Leading hypothesis replaces Main finding to make uncertainty visible. Server-owned measurements, controls and conditional diagnostic branches remain unchanged.

One paid v16 response started tentatively, then asserted low/modest scores and causal effects without the required traces. This was a semantic failure despite initial structural acceptance. A new regression guard checks the entire explanation/alternative rather than allowing a tentative opening to authorize later factual sentences. A single general mechanism can still be framed as a hypothesis by Sidekick; unsupported numerical values, observations, causal proof, scope errors and unavailable-record inferences remain rejected. The original v16 answer is an immutable negative fixture and retains only its diagnostic after the repair.
